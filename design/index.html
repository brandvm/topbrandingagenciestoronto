Use serif contrast and room to breathe.

Observed
A centred serif statement combines upright and italic words. A small accent button and a strip of distinct images sit within an airy cream-and-green composition.
Adapt
Reserve italics for emphasis, give the headline a controlled width and use a visual strip to punctuate long stretches of type.
Apply to
Toronto: expressive serif and asymmetric story rhythm. Best Branding Companies: balanced headline with selective italics.

Give editorial cards a lighter frame.

Observed
Two stories share a wide ruled frame. Small illustrations, generous gaps and a serif excerpt contrast with sans-serif titles and compact author rows.
Adapt
Use rules and space instead of heavy card backgrounds. Separate the title, excerpt and reading action through typography.

Make every article feel like a story.

Observed
The archive has an explicit year heading, article metadata, a prominent linked title and a substantial editorial image. Stories follow a familiar vertical sequence.
Adapt
Give titles, available dates and cover imagery a consistent hierarchy. Keep archives easy to read and move through.

Give the publication a memorable masthead.

Observed
An oversized publication title anchors the top left. Below it, a broad feature shares the page with a narrower list of stories separated by fine rules.

08

Keep the design useful

These rules apply through implementation and future repository edits.

Reading and interaction

  • One clear H1; logical H2/H3 structure and a keyboard skip link.
  • Visible focus; 44px design target for primary controls.
  • Stack grids and move the contents rail above the article on mobile.
  • Table overflow stays inside its labelled, keyboard-focusable region.
  • Support reduced motion and 200% zoom; preserve a readable comment panel.

Content and performance

  • 3 supplied articles; no filler content, invented authors or research dates.
  • Keep all ten agency profiles and ten FAQs per article.
  • Self-host fonts; use SVG icons and dimensioned responsive images.
  • Production budgets: 30KB compressed JS, 40KB compressed CSS, 150KB fonts per page.
  • Target Lighthouse 100; require performance median ≥95 and the other automated categories 100. Scores and field Core Web Vitals need separate measurement.
